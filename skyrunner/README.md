# Skyrunner

An arcade flight game over low-poly valleys and river canyons. Thread golden rings in a red-and-white prop plane over the open valley, or a fighter jet down the canyon levels: every ring you thread adds time to the clock. Touch the ground or run out of time and the flight is over.

- **Daily challenge:** one course a day, the same for every pilot. Fly it as often as you like; your best run goes on that day's leaderboard, and your friends hear about it when you take the lead.
- **Levels:** twelve low-level canyon runs that get narrower, deeper and faster, with a hand-built level 6 (one hard 90° corner, a pull-up over the ridge, then a dive onto a target) and a final mission: race down a twisting canyon, pop up over the ridge, dive onto a red target ring, then pull up hard over the mountains. From level 3, blue rings must be flown through under 200 km/h (they end tight hairpins) and from level 4 purple rings need over 330 km/h, so flooring it is not the answer. Finish a course to earn up to three stars (hit every ring for all three). Every level is open from the start, so jump to any canyon you like.
- **Free flight:** an endless course that ramps up the longer you last, with its own all-time board.
- **Controls:** a thumb stick on the left banks and pitches the plane, and a slider on the right sets your speed from about 100 to 400 km/h. Slow down to turn tighter, speed up to reach rings sooner. On a keyboard, arrow keys or WASD steer, Shift/E and Ctrl/Q change speed, and Space takes off.
- **Ideal path:** turn on "Ideal path" beside the stick to see a dashed line through the next rings. Flights take off at full throttle, so ease the slider back before tight turns.
- **Your progress is saved:** stars, daily bests and your free-flight record stay with you, and a flight cut short by closing the app still counts.
